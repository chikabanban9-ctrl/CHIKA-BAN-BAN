Our Journey

Our Story

CHIKA BAN BAN was born from a simple realization: the biggest joys often come in the smallest packages. We started with a mission to revolutionize how families hydrate, stripping away the bulk of traditional juices to focus on what truly matters—pure, explosive fruit flavor.

Our journey began in a small kitchen, experimenting with natural essences to create a concentrate that didn't just taste like fruit, but felt like a celebration. We wanted something that could travel from the beach to the backyard without the weight of heavy bottles.

Today, we're proud to bring that massive splash of fun to homes everywhere. One tiny 5g packet at a time, we're proving that you don't need a lot of space to make a huge impact on your day.
